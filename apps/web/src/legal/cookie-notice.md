# Cookie notice

*Draft for neon1.tech/1NEON/cookies. Effective date: [date of publication].*

**neon1.tech** sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

**The 1NEON portal** uses only the cookies it needs to work:

| Cookie | Purpose | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `__Secure-1neon_at` | Keeps you signed in (access token) | 15 minutes |
| `__Secure-1neon_rt` | Renews your sign-in without asking again | Up to 30 days, or until you sign out |
| Sign-in service cookies on auth.neon1.tech | Protect the sign-in step (Amazon Cognito) | Your sign-in session |

These cookies are strictly necessary, can only be read by the portal's own server, are never used for advertising or analytics, and are never shared. Because of that, the portal doesn't show a cookie consent banner. If you block them, you won't be able to sign in.

The portal stores nothing else in your browser. Preferences, such as your theme, are saved with your account.

If I ever add optional cookies, such as analytics, I'll update this notice and ask for your consent first.
